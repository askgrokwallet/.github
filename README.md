AI agents can think, trade, and pay. AskGrokWallet is the layer that decides what they are allowed to do — inside human-defined rules, with a verifiable receipt for every outcome.

- **Policy engine** — plain-English rules compiled to `allow / ask / deny`
- **Approval inbox** — human in the loop, one tap to decide
- **Signed receipts** — who, what, how much, who approved, anchored onchain
- **Any rail, any agent** — Stripe Link, Ramp, Phantom, Circle, Kraken, or our Boundless vault; Grok Bot, Cursor, Claude, Grok Build
